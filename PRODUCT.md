# Redwood Escapes — Product Context

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
A vacation planning and inspiration site for California's Redwood National and State Parks. Helps travelers discover trails, parks, camping, lodging, and wildlife before and during their trip.

## Target Users
- **Vacation planners** (primary): adults 28–55 researching a trip to the redwoods, comparing options, building an itinerary. Desktop-first, thorough, price-conscious.
- **On-trip visitors**: already en route, looking up a specific trail or park on mobile.
- **Dream browsers**: not yet committed, need emotional inspiration to pull them toward booking.

## Emotional Goals
Awe. Smallness. Ancient scale. The feeling of stepping outside of ordinary time. The site should make the forest feel real and close, not like a brochure.

## Brand Personality
Ancient / Wild / Earned (not curated comfort)

## Anti-References
- Generic booking sites (Expedia, Booking.com) — transactional, cold, grid-heavy
- Pinterest-style nature inspiration — too soft, too pastel
- NPS.gov — accurate but bureaucratic, no soul
- Safari/luxury glamping sites — too polished, removes the wildness

## Design Principles
1. Let the photography carry the emotion — don't fight it with heavy UI chrome
2. Hierarchy through scale contrast, not decoration
3. Whitespace that breathes like forest air
4. Navigation that feels like orientation, not wayfinding in a mall
