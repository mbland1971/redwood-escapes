Prairie Creek Redwoods State Park

Prairie Creek Redwoods State Park is, for many visitors, the beating heart of the redwood parks experience. It packs an extraordinary density of wonders into its 14,000 acres: the surreal green world of Fern Canyon, the wild sweep of Gold Bluffs Beach, the Newton B. Drury Scenic Parkway threading through cathedral-scale old growth, and Elk Prairie — one of the most reliable Roosevelt elk viewing spots in North America.

The park straddles the transition between ocean and ancient forest, offering experiences that most parks of its size couldn't hope to match. In a single day you can walk inside a 50-foot fern-covered canyon, watch a bull elk bugle in a misty meadow, and stand on a wild Pacific beach with no other humans in sight.

Prairie Creek was among the first parks established specifically to protect old-growth redwood forest. Its core groves have never been logged. What you see here is what all of California's northern coast looked like before commercial timber operations arrived in the 1850s.

What to See & Do

Key Attractions

Fern Canyon

The park's most famous attraction — a 50-foot deep gorge with walls blanketed in ancient ferns, featured as a filming location in Jurassic Park: The Lost World. Short walk with stream crossings.

Explore Trail →

Newton B. Drury Scenic Parkway

A 10-mile drive through cathedral old growth with the Big Tree Wayside loop, Elk Prairie, and pull-outs throughout. The most accessible old-growth experience in California.

Explore Parkway →

Gold Bluffs Beach

A wild, undeveloped Pacific beach backed by golden sandstone bluffs and bordered by redwood forest. Roosevelt elk frequently graze here at dawn. High-clearance vehicle recommended.

Elk Prairie

One of the most reliable elk viewing spots in North America. Year-round herds, with the dramatic fall rut (Sept–Nov) drawing photographers from around the world.

James Irvine Trail

A 10-mile round-trip through the park's finest old growth, connecting the visitor center to Fern Canyon. One of the great redwood hikes — rewarding, quiet, and spectacular.

Prairie Creek Campground

75 sites nestled in old-growth forest, year-round, with ranger-led programs in summer. One of the best campground locations in the entire redwood park system.

Elk Safety

Roosevelt elk weigh up to 1,100 pounds and are wild animals. Always maintain 75+ feet of distance. Never approach cows with calves or bulls during the fall rut. Exit your vehicle slowly and quietly when elk are near the roadway.

Insider Tips

  • • Visit Fern Canyon early morning for the best light
  • • Bring water shoes for Fern Canyon stream crossings
  • • Morning fog on the parkway is magical — embrace it
  • • Fall (Sept–Oct) offers elk rut and golden light
  • • Combine with Redwood National Park for a full day
